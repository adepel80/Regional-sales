# Hip-Hop Artist Performance Analysis | YouTube Analytics Dashboard

# Objective of the Analysis

The primary objective of this analysis is to evaluate and understand the YouTube performance of selected hip-hop artists by analyzing key metrics such as total views, likes, comments, subscribers, number of songs, release patterns, and collaborations. The analysis aims to identify trends in audience engagement, differences in artist performance, high-performing songs, seasonal and yearly changes in viewership, and the relationship between content volume and audience reach. It also seeks to determine which artists and songs generate the strongest audience response, identify the periods and days associated with higher content activity and engagement, and provide data-driven insights that can support better content planning, artist performance evaluation, and digital marketing decisions.

# Detailed Insights from the Hip-Hop Artist Performance Analysis

### Overall YouTube Performance: 
The analysis shows that the selected hip-hop artists generated approximately 4.95 billion total views, 41.89 million likes, and 2.18 million comments across 476 songs, while the artists collectively have approximately 22.29 million subscribers. This indicates that the dataset represents a substantial level of audience reach and engagement on YouTube.

### Artist Subscriber Performance: 
Rema has the highest subscriber count at approximately 5.7 million, followed by Davido with 5.1 million and Wizkid with 3.7 million. This indicates that the largest subscriber audiences are concentrated among a few artists, while Fola has the smallest subscriber base at approximately 0.2 million.

### Artist View Performance: 
Rema records the highest total views at approximately 1.2 billion, followed by Ayra Star with about 958 million views and Asake with approximately 844 million views. This shows that high YouTube viewership is concentrated among several leading artists, although subscriber size does not always correspond directly with total views.

### Difference Between Subscribers and Views: 
The comparison between subscribers and views shows that having a large subscriber base does not necessarily guarantee the highest total views. For example, Davido has approximately 5.1 million subscribers, while Ayra Star has approximately 3.4 million subscribers, yet Ayra Star records substantially more views in the dashboard. This demonstrates the importance of analyzing multiple performance indicators rather than relying solely on subscriber count.

### Monthly View Performance: 
YouTube views vary considerably across the months, with March recording the highest monthly performance at approximately 871 million views, followed by September at about 781 million views. The substantial difference between high- and low-performing months suggests that audience activity and content performance fluctuate significantly throughout the year.

### Annual View Performance:
The analysis shows a significant increase in total views from 2019 to 2022, with 2022 recording the highest annual view count of approximately 1.58 billion views. After reaching this peak, annual views declined but remained at relatively substantial levels in subsequent years.

### Annual Comment Performance:
2022 also recorded the highest number of comments at approximately 1.0 million, considerably higher than the surrounding years. The simultaneous peak in views and comments during 2022 indicates that this period experienced particularly strong audience activity within the dataset.

### Song Release Pattern:
Out of the 476 songs analyzed, 237 were released on Friday, making Friday the dominant release day and accounting for almost half of all songs in the dataset. Thursday follows with 60 songs, while Wednesday accounts for 42 songs, showing that song releases are heavily concentrated toward the end of the working week.

### Likes by Weekday:
Friday also records the highest number of likes at approximately 18 million, considerably higher than the other days of the week. This indicates that Friday content represents a substantial proportion of overall audience interaction, although the dashboard alone does not establish that releasing songs on Friday directly causes higher engagement.

### Top-Performing Songs:
“Calm Down” records approximately 724 million views, making it the highest-viewed song displayed in the analysis, followed by “Rush” with approximately 607 million views and “Essence” with approximately 372 million views. These results demonstrate that a relatively small number of highly successful songs can account for a significant portion of the overall view volume.

### Artist Collaboration Performance:
Davido has the highest number of featured appearances, with approximately 60 features, followed by Fireboy DML with 35 and Wizkid with 31. This indicates that collaboration is a significant part of the content represented in the dataset, although the dashboard does not by itself establish whether a higher number of collaborations leads to higher views.

### Number of Songs by Artist: 
The number of songs is relatively similar across most of the artists, with Ayra Star, Davido, Fireboy DML, Rema, and Wizkid each having approximately 70 songs in the dataset. Asake has approximately 67 songs, while Fola has 59. This relatively balanced content volume provides a useful basis for comparing artist performance beyond simply looking at the number of songs released.

### Engagement Relative to Reach:
The dashboard shows a substantial difference between total views and active engagement, with 4.95 billion views compared with 41.89 million likes and 2.18 million comments. This indicates that while the content reaches a very large audience, only a relatively small proportion of viewers actively engage through likes and comments.

### Content Performance Concentration:
The large differences between the highest-performing songs and the rest of the catalog suggest that overall artist performance is influenced significantly by individual hit songs. This means that analyzing only total artist views may hide the contribution of individual high-performing songs.

### Overall Business Insight: 
Overall, the analysis demonstrates that YouTube performance among the selected hip-hop artists varies considerably across artists, songs, months, years, release days, and engagement metrics. The findings show that audience reach is concentrated among leading artists and highly successful songs, while content releases and engagement are particularly concentrated around Fridays and certain high-performing periods.
